- Delivered `/e/nurture` as a mobile employee nurture workbench with persisted customer segmentation: ongoing nurturing, repurchase opportunities and dormant reactivation. The page shows order/task signals, next contact timing, loading/empty/error/forbidden/success states, retiering, touchpoint capture and optional follow-up task creation.
